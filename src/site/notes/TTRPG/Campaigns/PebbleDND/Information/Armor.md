# Overview
Armor determines the overall Armor Class of a person and some even boost certain stats.
### Properties
- Armor Class -> Self explanatory
- Influence -> Stat changes the equipment provides
- Special -> Unique abilities provided by the equipment
## Basic
#### Brotherhood of Iron Armor
> AC11 | Dex & Con -1
#### Scrapshield Office Suit
> AC11 | +1 to all Defensive action checks
#### R.B. Chef Garbs
> AC11
#### R.B. Sous Chef Garbs
> AC11 | Heal 3 HP every 4 turns
#### R.B. Chef de Cuisine
> AC12
> After killing an enemy, heal for 2 HP (4 HP if enemy had bleed).
> The last kill in the encounter heals everyone in Chef attire by 2 HP
#### Gyeong-Mi's Outfit
> AC10 | Str +2 & Con +2
