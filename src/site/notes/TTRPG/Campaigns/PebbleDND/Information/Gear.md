# Overview
Gear is additional equipment with a wide variety of minor buffs. Up to 3 gears may be equipped at a time.
## Basic
#### Scrap Shield
> AC +1
#### Looter Bag
> Gain 3 Ahn for each defeated enemy after an encounter 
## Workshop
#### W. Corp Hat
> Gain 1 charge at turn start
## EGO
#### Perception Blocking Mask
> AC +1
> Become unhittable and use two actions for the first turn (+2 damage with said actions)
