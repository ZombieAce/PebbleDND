# Fixer
Despite this being a race, many have come to understand it's but a human profession. There are few who are **true fixers**, with such blood running through their vains. They have naturally unwavering willpower and a strong survival instinct.
### Passives
- **Rat's Guide to the Backstreets** -> Status effects are more potent based on the current Fixer Grade
- [LVL 2] **Shin** -> If **Mang** check is successful gain **Shin** for 3 turns -> AC +2
### Unique Skills
- **Focus Strikes** -> DC 12 | 3d6 pierce
- [LVL 2] **Mang** -> [Single Use] Wis DC15 | For 1 turn add an extra hit die for every **Mang**
	- Gain an extra ring for every 5 levels (e.g. lvl 5, 10, 15, 20) (Max. 5 Rings)

# Bloodfiend
Born from pure bloodthirst, they hide from society and strike from the shadows only to feed on the ever delicious human blood. They are few in numbers but get stronger the more they drink and the higher they are in the familial hierarchy.
### Passives
- **Hematic Regeneration** -> If a max die was rolled on an attack, regen 1d4 HP
### Unique Skills
- **Blood Manipulation** -> DC 12 | Can use stored blood to manifest **Hemoweapons**
	- Blood Scythe -> 2d6 slash
	- Blood Gauntlets -> 2d6 blunt
	- Blood Rapier -> 2d6 pierce

# God
Not much is known about this race, but one thing is clear: Unlike what their name suggests, they are no gods.
### Passives
- **Obsidian Skin** -> +4 HP, +3 Init
- **Nail** -> Hit an enemy at least once to apply permanent debuff (take 1 damage per turn)
- **Hammer** -> When falling below 50% HP -> roll a d20 (DC 14) -> All allies heal 2 HP & +1 to a chosen stat but said allies can only target nailed enemies
### Unique Skills
- **Play the System** -> DC12 (If nat20 use **Play Around the System** instead) | Roll a d4
	- 1 -> Rewind an ally (heal 2 HP)
	- 2 -> Rewind attack (next hit gets an extra attack die)
	- 3 -> Collapse (Debuff the enemies needed roll, making it need to be 2 higher for a succesful hit)
	- 4 -> Played (Rewind cooldowns)
- **Play Around the System**
	- 1 -> Revive an ally (20% HP)
	- 2 -> Replays last team attack (Can't change target)
	- 3 -> Skip enemy turn
	- 4 -> Rewind ALL allies' cooldowns

# Flowerborn
Born as a result of interacting with [[TTRPG/Campaigns/PebbleDND/Information/City Hazards#The Golden Flower\|The Golden Flower]] or very rarily through natural means. Can come in ~~seven~~ six different variants.

-> **Green**: Medicine checks have their **DC** reduced by 4
-> **Orange**: Every turn of a battle the **DC** of your skills and weapon are reduced by 1 
-> **Yellow**: When an ally takes damage from an enemy, inflict +**1d4** damage towards that enemy
-> **Blue**: Passively heal the team 1-3 HP based on your roll (15-17 -> <span style="color:rgb(143, 240, 194)">1 HP</span> | 18-19 -> <span style="color:rgb(143, 240, 194)">2 HP</span> | 20 -> <span style="color:rgb(143, 240, 194)">3 HP</span>)
-> **Aqua**: Passively injure enemies that hit you for 2 damage
-> **Violet**: If hitting an enemy you already hit, Deal +1 damage
-> [Unlocked by encountering the Golden Flower] **Golden**
	-> Leaf it to me: Always first in initiative
	-> No No No: If hit, if last roll was above 15 reduce dmg by 2
	-> Fake Statistics: If hit more than 5 times, can choose to reroll 5 times for a nat20 (In those 5 rolls no matter the die is an attack, if nat20 is rolled before, it stops, but if rolled at 5 follow into LAST Jarona (5 dmg)
	-> Voicelines: Cha +4
	-> Flower Man: All stats +2 (besides Cha) and unlock new moves
### Passives
- Based on the variant as stated above
### Unique Skills
- [Must be Golden] **OMEGA** -> Obtain all the passives from the other flowerborn variants for the duration of the battle
- **Jarona!** -> Close the distance between an enemy & 1d6 blunt
- **Jarona Blitz** -> Close the distance between an enemy & 3d6 blunt
- [Must be Golden] **Prism Blow** -> Cha +10 & Enemies are unable to fight back for 1 turn
### Damage Vulnerabilities
- Fire
### Damage Resistances
- Water
### Damage Immunities
- None
### Condition Immunities
- None
