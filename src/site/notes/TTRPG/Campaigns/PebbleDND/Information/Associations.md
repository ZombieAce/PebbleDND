# Overview
**Associations** (also known as **The Wings**) are the main lawful forces in [[TTRPG/Campaigns/PebbleDND/Information/The City\|The City]]. They abide by The City's regulations and laws and work towards keeping it stable and functional. Each association plays an important role in The City's survival and serve a specific function.

They often have subsidiary [[TTRPG/Campaigns/PebbleDND/Information/Offices\|Offices]].
## Hana Association
- The 1st Association (Korean)
- Leader of the Associations; grades all [[TTRPG/Campaigns/PebbleDND/Information/Fixers\|Fixers]], [[TTRPG/Campaigns/PebbleDND/Information/Offices\|Offices]], and [[TTRPG/Campaigns/PebbleDND/Information/City Hazards\|City Hazards]]
## Zwei Association
- The 2nd Association (German)
- Specializes in public safety and defense, acting as a police force
## Tres Association
- The 3rd Association (Spanish)
- Manages [[TTRPG/Campaigns/PebbleDND/Information/Workshops\|Workshops]] and reviews [[TTRPG/Campaigns/PebbleDND/Information/Weaponry#Workshop\|Workshop weaponry]] for use by [[TTRPG/Campaigns/PebbleDND/Information/Fixers\|Fixers]] 
## Shi Association
- The 4th Association (Japanese)
- Specialized in stealth and assassination missions
## Cinq Association
- The 5th Association (French)
- Specializes in dueling. This is close to public entertainment.
## Liu Association
- The 6th Association (Chinese)
- Specializes in direct combat and warfare
## Seven Association
- The 7th Association (English)
- Specializes in information collection
## "Eight" Association
- The 8th Association (Unknown)
- This is a mysterious association with unknown functions and members. It is currently theorized it's specialized in espionage and infiltration and the association equivalent of [[TTRPG/Campaigns/PebbleDND/Information/The Five Fingers#The Pinky\|The Pinky]]
## Devyat Association
- The 9th Association (Russian)
- Specialize in transportation and delivery
## Dieci Association
- The 10th Association (Italian)
- Specialize in knowledge gathering and charity
## Öufi Association
- The 11th Association (Swiss German)
- Oversees trades, deals and internal affairs
