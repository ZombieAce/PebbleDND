# Overview
**The City** is the main area where human civilization exists in this world. Although that sounds like it would be a safe haven for humans, it is quite the contrary. This place is dangerous. Criminal activity is not uncommon and death is natural.
Even with the existance of laws and [[TTRPG/Campaigns/PebbleDND/Information/Associations\|Associations]], it's a flawed system where the just are few and sin is abundant.
## Organization
**The City** is split into several Districts, each one being the center of operations for a given [[TTRPG/Campaigns/PebbleDND/Information/Corporations\|corporation]]. These corporations heavily influence the lifestyle of the residents due to the [[TTRPG/Campaigns/PebbleDND/Information/Singularities\|Singularities]] they possess.
There are 25 districts in total and the corporations controlling them are ordered alphabeticaly.

Aside from corporations, each District is split into two types of zones: **Backstreets** and **Nests**.
### Nests
Nests are the central areas of districts governed by corporations in conjunction with associations and are *generally* safe. Usually the people residing in nests are wealthier than the average person and are entitled to education.
### Backstreets
The Backstreets are the areas leftover that couldnt be turned into nests. They are lawless, brutal places to live in, and are governed by one of [[TTRPG/Campaigns/PebbleDND/Information/The Five Fingers\|The Five Fingers]] or any subsidiary Syndicate if powerful enough. The people living within are usually subject to exorbitant "protection fees" and struggle to get by every day.

Many of the backstreets residents are envious of nest residents for living carefree and comfy while they struggle to survive on a daily basis.
