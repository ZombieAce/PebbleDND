# Overview
**City Hazards** are, as the name implies, a danger towards [[TTRPG/Campaigns/PebbleDND/Information/The City\|The City]]'s wellbeing. These can be anything from a person, to a group of people, to phenomena. These hazards also have their own unique classifications, assigned by the [[TTRPG/Campaigns/PebbleDND/Information/Associations#Hana Association\|Hana Association]] as per the list below:
## Classifications
### Canard
These City Hazards are so weak that they can barely be called hazards but they needed a classification anyway, thus Canard came to be. **Grade 9 Fixers** in general should be enough to deal with such cases.
