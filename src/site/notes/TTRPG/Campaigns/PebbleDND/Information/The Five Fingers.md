# Overview
The Five Fingers are the leaders of the Syndicates. As the name suggests, they are 5 organizations named after each finger of a hand. Much like [[TTRPG/Campaigns/PebbleDND/Information/Associations\|Associations]] they also often have subsidiary [[TTRPG/Campaigns/PebbleDND/Information/Syndicates\|Syndicates]]
## The Thumb
They have a strong armory, equipped with guns and don outfits similar to Mafia. They have a strong sense of justice to the point where they are willing to cut off limbs as retribution for breaking the rules of their family.
## The Index
Guided by the **prescripts**, they follow every order given to them without question, no matter how gruesome it may be. The Index frequently hands out prescripts containing tasks that the recievers must do and if dismissed, Index Proselytes will be dispatched.
## The Middle
By far the most prominent finger, The Middle, holds strong values in family and even rank themselves with familial titles (Big Brother, Little Sister etc.). Their main fixation is **Vengeance**. If any of their family members get wronged, they will do anything to pursue and deliver retribution. 
## The Ring
They are obsessed with art. They will do anything to create and observe art, often resorting to murder. Unlike the others they prefer to work from the shadows.
## The Pinky
Little is known about this mysterious organization. Noone really knows who any of the members are. They are all scattered around [[TTRPG/Campaigns/PebbleDND/Information/The City\|The City]] and dont even know eachother. When the time is right, they will reconvene at meetings to fulfill a grand mission.
