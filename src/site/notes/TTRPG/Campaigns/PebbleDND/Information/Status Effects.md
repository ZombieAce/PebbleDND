## Positive Effects
#### Haste
> Increase initiative by count & +(count) to Dex checks (remove at turn end)
## Negative Effects
#### Bleed
> Take 1d4 of damage at turn start then reduce count
#### Burn
> Take (1d4+count) of damage and remove status 
#### Paralyzed
> Reduce all check rolls by 2
#### Bind
> Reduce initiative by count & -(count) to Dex checks (remove at turn end)
#### Disarmed
> Disable weapon for (count) turns
#### Fairy
> Unique to [[TTRPG/Campaigns/PebbleDND/Information/Races#Arbiter\|Arbiter]] | If an Arbiter exclusive move hits the target explode all Fairy stacks for (count)d4 **true damage** (No Kill)
