# Worldbuilding
It is said that humans are the apex predators of this planet. Thanks to their far superior intelligence compared to nature's other animals, they have been able to survive and multiply rapidly over the course of millenia. They remained on top of the food chain for a long, long time... Until.. [[TTRPG/Campaigns/PebbleDND/Information/The Outskirts#Creatures\|Creatures of the Outskirts]] started appearing. These beasts of various sizes and proportions wreaked havoc on human civilizations, mercilessly killing millions upon millions of people, destroying cities wherever they tread leaving nothing but remnants of a once thriving population in their path.

Eventually, humans were forced to retreat and successfully managed to build up humanity's last bastion known as [[TTRPG/Campaigns/PebbleDND/Information/The City\|The City]]. Although it was just an emergency fix, overtime it stabilized, fortified and became a safe haven where humans would be able to live peacefully outside of the [[TTRPG/Campaigns/PebbleDND/Information/The Outskirts#Creatures\|creature's]] reach. Or... That would be the case if not for humans' unbeatable urge to sin. Even in this fort that humanity has built up to survive, they continue to ruin, even more so than they used to on the outside. Most of them are fueled by **wrath**, **lust**, **gluttony**, **greed**, and **envy**. Murder is second nature to the average person here. This place where humans kill humans.. is no better than the outside.

You ([[TTRPG/Campaigns/PebbleDND/PebbleDND#Party Members\|The Party]]) wake up in a strange place... It's oddly warm, you see bookshelves stretch to the skies, you can feel the smell of fresh books in the air.. before you stands a woman with pale skin, short blue hair, sharp golden eyes and a serious, unamused expression. Before you could do anything you catch a glimpse of her smile as you lose focus and blackout.

As you come to, you realize you all passed out in some unknown alleyway. Much to your dismay, your belongings got stolen but at least you weren't killed.
