# Updating Health and Stats
Every level up you get to increase your health.

$Current Health + Average D10 + Con Modifier = New Health$

As for the stats, at level 1 you get to assign your 2 proficiency bonus points into the stats of your choosing and every 4 levels you get to reassign your increasing proficiency bonus (Level 4, 8, 12, 16, 20)

# Damage Types and Resistances
### Damage Types
There are 3 main damage types:
- Slash
- Pierce
- Blunt

At the DM's discression, if the circumstances are right, it may be possible to deal **true damage** which deals direct unmodified damage, bypassing resistances. This may rarely occur upon:
- Rolling a nat20
- Using special equipment
- Other valid cases as deemed by the DM

There are also special damage types such as fire or bleed (ref. [[TTRPG/Campaigns/PebbleDND/Information/Status Effects\|Status Effects]])
### Resistances
Resistances modify the incoming damage based on it's type. Some races even have natural resistances or vulnerabilities to certain damage types.

The resistances are as follows:
- Resistant -> Roll with disadvantage
- Vulnerable -> Roll with advantage
- Immune -> Nullify incoming damage

Resistances can also be attained by wearing specific equipment or using skills that modify these stats.
